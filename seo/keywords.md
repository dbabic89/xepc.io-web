# xepc.io keyword map (W0)

Date: 2026-10-22. Scope: BiH first, Croatia second; HR/BS phrasing, short EN and DE equivalents. Feeds W1 (home), W2a/W2b (subpages), Maqo landing.
No search-volume numbers appear anywhere: no source in this research showed any. "Evidence" means: Google autocomplete (AC), Google SERP top results (SERP), "Pretražuje se i sljedeće" related searches (REL), "People also ask" (PAA), competitor page. See "Method and limits" at the end before relying on any negative claim.

## Summary: primary phrase per page

| Page | Primary (HR/BS) | Why this one and not a neighbour |
|---|---|---|
| Home `/` | izrada softvera | Broad head term. AC under it offers "po narudžbi", "po mjeri", "cena". Subpages take the narrower modifiers, so home does not compete with them. |
| maqo.xepc.io | program za male firme | Matches what Maqo is (a package for a small company). AC-supported. SERP is Pantheon Small Business, RIVA, Ritam ERP, spona.io: same product class. |
| `/programiranje/` | programiranje po narudžbi | Bare "programiranje" is education / jobs / garage remotes (see below), so the head word is unusable. "Po narudžbi" returns commercial results. |
| `/izrada-web-stranica/` | izrada web stranica | Strongest AC of all pages: cijena, zagreb, sarajevo, split, rijeka... |
| `/izrada-mobilnih-aplikacija/` | izrada mobilnih aplikacija | AC + REL both support it; BiH agencies rank for it. |
| `/izrada-aplikacija-po-mjeri/` | izrada softvera po mjeri | AC-supported; "izrada aplikacija po mjeri" (the slug wording) gave no AC but has a SERP, so it is a secondary. |
| `/ai-rjesenja/` | ai integracija u poslovanje | HR SERP is dominated by agencies selling exactly this; AC "ai integracija" exists. |

Cannibalisation warning: "izrada softvera po narudžbi" (programiranje) and "izrada softvera po mjeri" (po-mjeri) return nearly the same top results (evolva.hr, magicmarinac.hr, dotsoft.hr, prolink.hr, syntax.ba). Google may treat them as one intent. Mitigation used here: programiranje targets the "developer for hire, any platform" intent; po-mjeri targets "internal business system"; the two link to each other. Whether this separation works is unverifiable before the pages are indexed.

Copy rules applied to every title/description/H1 below: no buzzwords, no exclamation marks, no emoji, no invented numbers or clients. Claims reused from the current site: "Odgovaramo u najkraćem mogućem roku", "rad u fazama", "pisana procjena troška prije početka", "SEO optimizacija u cijeni", "objava u trgovinama, rad bez interneta, održavanje".
Wording: "firma/firme" is used instead of "tvrtka" in titles because BiH queries use "firma" (AC: "ai automatizacija za firme", "ai chatbot za firmu"); the body copy can use tvrtka in HR and firma/preduzeće in BS variants (BS "preduzeće" got no AC evidence, see AI page).

## Method and limits

- Autocomplete: Google suggest endpoint (`suggestqueries.google.com`, `hl=hr`, `gl=ba` and `gl=hr`), plus `hl=de` and `hl=en`. The `gl` value had no visible effect: BA and HR runs returned identical lists for 48 identical queries. The lists are therefore "Google in HR language", not BiH-only or HR-only. Completions are an unranked list; autocomplete shows what Google suggests, not volume. An empty list does not mean nobody searches it (the endpoint returns nothing for many long phrases).
- SERPs: google.hr in the built-in browser with `gl=ba` (Google displayed the location as Sarajevo or Zenica, "based on previous activity", so results are partly personalised to this browser). About 30 result pages were read through same-origin fetches, 6 as live pages. Fetched HTML does not contain "People also ask" or related searches.
- Google then showed its "unusual traffic" CAPTCHA (/sorry) after about seven live loads. It was not bypassed or solved. Live related searches were captured only for "izrada web stranica" and "izrada mobilnih aplikacija"; live pages for "izrada softvera po mjeri", "izrada aplikacija po mjeri" and "programiranje po narudžbi" showed neither related searches nor PAA.
- PAA: no "People also ask" box was seen on any SERP read. This is a PARTIAL negative (fetched HTML cannot show it, and 6 live loads is a small sample). No PAA questions are quoted anywhere above for that reason.
- The WebSearch tool is US-only and returned directories and PDFs for these queries; it was used only for the price and AI-adoption context lines.
- Competitor titles, descriptions and H1s marked "verified" were fetched directly from the page on 2026-10-22. SERP URLs shown as `domain/path` come from the visible breadcrumb in the result and may be truncated.
- Not done: current Google position of xepc.io (a site: query would have risked the CAPTCHA); search volumes (no source shown any; Search Console, once O6 is done, will show real queries); Bing or other engines; Croatian city-level queries beyond autocomplete (Zagreb etc. appear in AC for web stranice only); FBiH-specific payroll/invoice terminology for Maqo.
